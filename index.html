In the Rhythm of the Sunnah

Unlike standard apps, your day does not reset at midnight, but traditionally at sunset (Maghrib) – automatically calculated based on your location.

Daily Adhkar Sessions

Recite your morning, evening, and sleep Adhkar in a fluid, flowing view. With the adjustable auto-mode, the app automatically transitions to the next Dhikr.

Modern Tasbih Counter & Custom Dhikr

Choose from authentic standard Tasbihs or easily create your own Dhikrs with personal goals. Features manual counting or precise auto-counting with BPM tempo control and haptic feedback.

Quran Reader

Read the Quran in the beautiful, authentic "KFGQPC Hafs Script" font. Includes pinch-to-zoom for seamless font size adjustment and automatic saving of your reading progress.

Surah Al-Kahf Friday Focus

Track your weekly reading of Surah Al-Kahf every Friday (from Thursday evening to Friday evening) with an integrated, optimized reader.

Fajr Alarm & Prayer Status

Set smart Fajr alarms that adapt automatically to the exact prayer time. Effortlessly document whether you prayed Fajr on time.

Local & Private

Your privacy is important to us. All settings, statistics, and entries are stored offline and securely on your device. No registration required, no tracking, and 100% ad-free.
